    Explain it to the people who decide
    
      
    Gemini calls our verified calculator for every figure, then explains the result for each audience. It never does the arithmetic itself.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Safety check on each option
    
      
    The Decision Gate blocks anything that raises total emissions or only moves pollution from air to water, however confident the model is.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    After the repair: verify the improvement
    
      
    Gemini compares the after photo with the original. An improvement is recorded only if it is the same component and the evidence supports it. This is an evidence record, not an official certification.
